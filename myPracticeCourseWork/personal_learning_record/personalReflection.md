# personal reflection

---
When I first began this module, I expected it to provide a curriculum that focused heavily on the hardware of a computer. My initial assumption was that we would delve deeply into the physical aspects of computer architecture, such as circuits, processors, and hardware integration. This expectation stemmed from my limited exposure to computer science as a degree and my preconceived notions about its foundational elements. While some aspects of hardware were covered, the module's scope and focus were broader than I anticipated, which brought a mix of challenges and revelations.

One of the most surprising aspects of the module was encountering material I had not anticipated. Specifically, I had never heard of GitHub before the first lesson. GitHub was introduced as a central tool for our coursework and while initially it presented a challenge, I quickly found it fascinating. The process of learning GitHub was not without its hurdles; I struggled to grasp its functionality at first. However, after dedicating time to independent research and watching tutorials, I gained a clearer understanding of its usefulness. As my familiarity grew, so did my appreciation for GitHub as a very useful tool, not just for this module but for my future academic and professional pursuits. The discovery of GitHub was both surprising and rewarding, transforming what initially felt like a challenge into a significant learning experience.

Another area that captured my interest was the lessons on different programming languages and their applications. These sessions broadened my understanding of how various languages are tailored for specific purposes, each with unique features and strengths. This exploration of programming languages was particularly engaging because it opened my eyes to the diverse ways software development can address different problems.

While much of the module introduced new concepts, there were parts that served as a recap of material I was already familiar with. For instance, I came into the course with a basic understanding of computer architecture. While this provided a foundation, the module expanded on my knowledge by introducing topics like the kernel and user processes, particularly within UNIX operating systems. These deeper insights added layers of complexity to my existing knowledge, bridging gaps I hadn't previously noticed.

However, not everything in the module was straightforward for me. Some of the practical lessons, particularly those involving CPUlator, left me feeling confused. This confusion stemmed from having missed a few sessions, which put me at a disadvantage. To address this gap, I turned to online resources, including YouTube tutorials and documentation, to familiarize myself with the operations and concepts being taught. While this self-directed learning helped me catch up, it also highlighted the importance of staying consistent with the practical lessons in my future academic journey.

Reflecting on the module, I feel that it provided a comprehensive and well-rounded curriculum. I cannot think of any significant gaps or topics that were overlooked. Additionally the module covered a wide range of material, offering insights into both theoretical concepts and practical applications. This balance made the learning experience dynamic and engaging.

In conclusion, my journey through this module has been one of growth and discovery. While I entered with expectations centred on the hardware aspect, the module’s broader scope expanded my knowledge of programming languages, and deepened my understanding of computer systems. Though there were moments of confusion, these challenges ultimately enhanced my learning. The module has provided me with a strong foundation that I am confident will support my future studies and career in the field of computer science.
